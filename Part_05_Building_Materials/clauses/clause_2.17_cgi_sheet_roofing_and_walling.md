# Section 2.17: Cgi Sheet Roofing and Walling

2.17  Cgi Sheet Roofing and Walling
Galvanized corrugated steel sheets conforming to BDS 868, Galvanized Corrugated
Sheet Roof and Wall Coverings, may be used over structural framing for construction of
roofs and walls. Requirements for various roofing materials including CGI sheet have
been specified in Sec 2.13 above.
