# Section 4.7.1.3.4: Fish Plates/Gusset Plated Joints

4.7.1.3.4 Fish Plates/Gusset Plated Joints
At least 25 mm thick hardwood splice plate or 12 mm thick structural grade plywood
are used. Solid bamboo pins help in fastening the assembly.
