# Section 6.3.12.3: For columns laterally supported on four sides by beams of approximately

6.3.12.3  For columns laterally supported on four sides by beams of approximately
equal depth or by slabs, it shall be permitted to base strength of the column on an
assumed concrete strength in the column joint equal to 75 percent of column concrete
strength plus 35 percent of floor concrete strength. In the application of Sec 6.3.12.3,
ratio of column concrete strength to slab concrete strength shall not be taken larger
than 2.5 in design.
