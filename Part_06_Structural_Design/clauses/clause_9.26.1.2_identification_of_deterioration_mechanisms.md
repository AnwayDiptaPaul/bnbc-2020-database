# Section 9.26.1.2: Identification of deterioration mechanisms

9.26.1.2 Identification of deterioration mechanisms
Deterioration of a structure is caused by the environmental actions and loading
conditions. Environment-oriented deterioration includes carbonation-induced
deterioration, chloride-induced deterioration, chemical attack, alkali-aggregate
reaction, etc. On the other hand external force-oriented deterioration includes
fatigue, excessive loading, and differential settlement of the support.
