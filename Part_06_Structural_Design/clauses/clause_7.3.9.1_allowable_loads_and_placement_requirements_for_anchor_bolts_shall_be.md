# Section 7.3.9.1: Allowable  loads and  placement requirements for anchor bolts shall be

7.3.9.1 Allowable  loads and  placement requirements for anchor bolts shall be
in accordance with the following:
(a) Bent bar anchor bolts shall have a hook with a 90o bend with an inside
diameter of 301 plus an extension of 1.501 at the free end.
(b) Headed anchor bolts shall have a standard bolt head.
(c) Plate anchor bolts shall have a plate welded to the shank to provide
anchorage equivalent to headed anchor bolts.
