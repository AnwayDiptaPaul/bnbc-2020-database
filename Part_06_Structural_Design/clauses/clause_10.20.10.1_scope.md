# Section 10.20.10.1: Scope

10.20.10.1  Scope
Intermediate moment frames (IMF) are expected to withstand limited inelastic
deformations in their members and connections when subjected to the forces resulting
from the motions of the design earthquake. IMF shall meet the requirements in this Section.
