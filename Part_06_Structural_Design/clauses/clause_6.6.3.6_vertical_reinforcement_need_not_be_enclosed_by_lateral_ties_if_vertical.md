# Section 6.6.3.6: Vertical reinforcement need not be enclosed by lateral ties if vertical

6.6.3.6 Vertical reinforcement need not be enclosed by lateral ties if vertical
reinforcement area is not greater than 0.01 times gross concrete area, or where
vertical reinforcement is not required as compression reinforcement.
