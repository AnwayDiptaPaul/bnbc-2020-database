# Section 9.15.2: Special Anchorage Devices

9.15.2 Special Anchorage Devices
Where special anchorage devices are to be used, supplemental skin
reinforcement shall be furnished in the corresponding regions of the anchorage
zone, in addition to the confining reinforcement specified for the anchorage
device. This supplemental reinforcement shall be similar in configuration and at
least equivalent in volumetric ratio to any supplementary skin reinforcement
used in the qualifying acceptance tests of the anchorage device.
