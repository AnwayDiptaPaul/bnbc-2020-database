# Section 13.3.1.2: Design effective width of concrete

13.3.1.2 Design effective width of concrete
The effective width of the concrete slab is the sum of the effective widths for each side of
the beam centerline, each of which shall not exceed:
(a) one-eighth of the beam span, center-to-center of supports;
(b) one-half the distance to the centerline of the adjacent beam; or
(c) the distance to the edge of the slab.
