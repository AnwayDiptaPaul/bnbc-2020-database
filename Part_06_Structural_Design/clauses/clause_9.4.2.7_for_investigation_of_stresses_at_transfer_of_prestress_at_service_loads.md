# Section 9.4.2.7: For investigation of stresses at transfer of prestress, at service loads,

9.4.2.7 For investigation of stresses at transfer of prestress, at service loads,
and at cracking loads, elastic theory shall be used with the following
assumptions:
(i)
Strains vary linearly with depth through the entire load range.
(ii) At cracked sections, concrete resists no tension.

9.4.4
Shapes of Beams and Girders
For prestressed concrete non-composite beams/girders, the frequently used
shapes are:
(a) Symmetrical I-section,
(b) Unsymmetrical I-section,
(c) T-section,
(d) Inverted T-section,
(e) Box section and
(f)
Solid/hollow rectangular section.
Commentary:
The suitability of selecting a particular shape will depend on the specific design
requirement and economy of construction. In general, T or equal or unequal I-
section are common choices to achieve economy in steel and concrete. Due
consideration to the simplicity of formwork is also required.
