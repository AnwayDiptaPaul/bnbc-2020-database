# Section 6.10.10.1: A precast element to be made composite with cast-in-place concrete

6.10.10.1 A precast element to be made composite with cast-in-place concrete
shall be permitted to be tested in flexure as a precast element alone in
accordance with Sections 6.10.10.1.1 and 6.10.10.1.2.
