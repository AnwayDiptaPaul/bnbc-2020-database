# Section 4.7.1.2: Modern practices

4.7.1.2 Modern practices
Following are some of the modern practices for bamboo jointing (Figure 6.4.10):
(a) Plywood or solid timber gusset plates maybe used at joint assemblies of web
and chord connection in a truss and fixed with bamboo pins or bolts. Hollow
cavities of bamboo need to be stuffed with wooden plugs.
(b) Use of wooden inserts to reinforce the ends of the bamboo before forming
the joints. Alternatively steel bands clamps with integral bolt/eye may be
fitted around bamboo sections for jointing.
