# Section 6.5.6.9: Factored moments in columns and walls

6.5.6.9 Factored moments in columns and walls
6.5.6.9.1
Columns and walls built integrally with a slab system shall resist
moments caused by factored loads on the slab system.
6.5.6.9.2
At an interior support, supporting elements above and below the slab
shall resist the factored moment specified by Eq. 6.6.87 in direct proportion to their
stiffnesses unless a general analysis is made.
