# Section 9.4.5.1: Concrete Preparation and Design: Concrete shall be prepared, conveyed,

9.4.5.1 Concrete Preparation and Design: Concrete shall be prepared, conveyed,
placed/cast, cured, tested and maintained following appropriate sections of
Chapter 5 of the Code. Relevant applicable standards are mentioned in Chapter
5 and also listed in Table 6.9.9. Unless specifically applicable to prestressed
concrete, general design requirements of normal concrete are those of Chapter
6 of the Code.
