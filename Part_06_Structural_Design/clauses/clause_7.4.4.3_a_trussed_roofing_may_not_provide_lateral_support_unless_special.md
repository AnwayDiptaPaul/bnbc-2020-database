# Section 7.4.4.3: A trussed roofing may not provide lateral support unless special

7.4.4.3  A trussed roofing may not provide lateral support unless special
measures are adopted to brace and anchor the roofing. However, in case of
residential and similar buildings of conventional design with trussed roofing
having cross walls, it may be assumed that stability requirements are met  by
the cross walls and structural analysis for stability may be dispensed with.
