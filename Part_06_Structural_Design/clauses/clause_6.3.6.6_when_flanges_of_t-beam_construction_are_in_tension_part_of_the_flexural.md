# Section 6.3.6.6: When flanges of T-beam construction are in tension, part of the flexural

6.3.6.6 When flanges of T-beam construction are in tension, part of the flexural
tension reinforcement shall be distributed over an effective flange width as defined in
Sec 6.1.13, or a width equal to one-tenth the span, whichever is smaller. If the
effective flange width exceeds one-tenth the span, some longitudinal reinforcement
shall be provided in the outer portions of the flange.
