# Section 11.11.2.2: The fingers can be cut from edge-to-edge or from face-to-face. The difference

11.11.2.2 The fingers can be cut from edge-to-edge or from face-to-face. The difference
is mainly in appearance, although bending strength increases if several fingers share the
load. Thus a joist is slightly stronger with edge-to-edge finger joints and a plank is stronger
with face-to-face finger joint.
