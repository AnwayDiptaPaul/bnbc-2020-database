# Section 4.7: Design and Techniques of Joints

4.7 Design and Techniques of Joints
4.7.1
Bamboo Joints
Round, tubular form of bamboo requires an approach different to that used for sawn
timber. Susceptibility to crushing at the open ends, splitting tendency, variation in
diameter, wall thickness and straightness are some of the associated issues which
have to be taken care of while designing and detailing the connections with bamboo.
