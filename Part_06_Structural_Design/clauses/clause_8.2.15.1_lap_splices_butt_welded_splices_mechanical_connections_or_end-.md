# Section 8.2.15.1: Lap splices, butt welded splices, mechanical connections, or end-

8.2.15.1 Lap splices, butt welded splices, mechanical connections, or end-
bearing splices shall be used with the limitations of Sections 8.2.15.2 to 8.2.15.4
below. A splice shall satisfy the requirements for all load combinations for the
column.
