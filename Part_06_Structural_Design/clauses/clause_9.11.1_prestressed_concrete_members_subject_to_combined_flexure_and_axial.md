# Section 9.11.1: Prestressed Concrete Members Subject to Combined Flexure and Axial

9.11.1 Prestressed Concrete Members Subject to Combined Flexure and Axial
Load
With or without non-prestressed reinforcement, Prestressed concrete members
subject to combined flexure and axial load shall be proportioned by the strength
design methods of this Code. Effects of prestress, creep, shrinkage, and
temperature change shall be included.
