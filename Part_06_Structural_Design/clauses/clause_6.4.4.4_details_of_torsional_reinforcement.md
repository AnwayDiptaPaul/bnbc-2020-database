# Section 6.4.4.4: Details of torsional reinforcement

6.4.4.4 Details of torsional reinforcement
6.4.4.4.1
Torsion reinforcement shall consist of longitudinal bars or tendons and
one or more of the following:
(a) Closed stirrups or closed ties, perpendicular to the axis of the member;
(b) A closed cage of welded wire reinforcement with transverse wires
perpendicular to the axis of the member;
(c) Spiral reinforcement.
6.4.4.4.2
Transverse torsional reinforcement shall be anchored by one of the
following:
(a) A 135o standard hook, or seismic hook as defined in Sec 8.1.2.1(d) Chapter
8, around a longitudinal bar;
(b) According to Sec 8.2.10.2 Chapter 8 in regions where the concrete
surrounding the anchorage is restrained against spalling by a flange or slab
or similar member.
6.4.4.4.3
Longitudinal torsion reinforcement shall be developed at both ends.
