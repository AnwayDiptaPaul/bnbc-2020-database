# Section 6.4.7.6: At front face of bracket or corbel, primary tension reinforcement shall be

6.4.7.6 At front face of bracket or corbel, primary tension reinforcement shall be
anchored by one of the following:
(a) By a structural weld to a transverse bar of at least equal size; weld to be
designed to develop  of primary tension reinforcement;
(b) By bending primary tension reinforcement back to form a horizontal loop; or
(c) By some other means of positive anchorage.
