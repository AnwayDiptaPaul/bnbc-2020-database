# Section 3.10.6.11: Drilled shaft in layered soil profile

3.10.6.11 Drilled shaft in layered soil profile
The short-term settlement of shafts in a layered soil profile may be estimated by
summing the proportional settlement components from layers of cohesive and
cohesionless soil comprising the subsurface profile.
