# Section 7.3.9.2: The effective embedment length, ‡1 for bent bar anchors shall be the

7.3.9.2 The effective embedment length, ‡1 for bent bar anchors shall be the
length of embedment measured perpendicular from the surface of the masonry
to the bearing surface of the bent end minus one anchor bolt diameter. For plate
or headed anchor bolts ‡1 shall be the length of embedment measured
perpendicular from the surface of the masonry to the bearing surface of the
plate or head of the anchorage. All bolts shall be grouted in place with at least 25
mm of grout between the bolt and the masonry except that 6 mm diameter bolts
may be placed in bed joints which are at least twice as thick as the diameter of
the bolt.
