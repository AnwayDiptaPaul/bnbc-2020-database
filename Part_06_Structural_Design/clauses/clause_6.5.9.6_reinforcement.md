# Section 6.5.9.6: Reinforcement

6.5.9.6 Reinforcement
The recommendations given in Sec 8.1.6 Chapter 8 regarding maximum distance
between bars apply to areas of solid concrete in this form of construction. The
curtailment, anchorage and cover to reinforcement shall be as specified below:
(a) At least 50 percent of the total main reinforcement shall be carried through
the bottom on to the bearing and anchored in accordance with Sec 8.2.8
Chapter 8.
(b) Where a slab, which is continuous over supports, has been designed as
simply supported, reinforcement shall be provided over the support to
control cracking. This reinforcement shall have a cross-sectional area of not
less than one quarter of that required in the middle of the adjoining spans
and shall extend at least one-tenth of the clear span into adjoining spans.
In slabs with permanent blocks, the side cover to the reinforcement shall not
be less than 10 mm. In all other cases, cover shall be provided according to
Sec 8.1.7 Chapter 8.
