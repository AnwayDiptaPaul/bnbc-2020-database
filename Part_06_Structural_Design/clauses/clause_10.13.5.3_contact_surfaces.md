# Section 10.13.5.3: Contact surfaces

10.13.5.3 Contact surfaces
Paint is permitted in bearing-type connections. For slip-critical connections, the faying
surface requirements shall be in accordance with the RCSC Specification for Structural
Joints Using ASTM A325 or A490 Bolts, Section 3.2.2(b).
