# Section 10.20.13.1: Scope

10.20.13.1 Scope
Special concentrically braced frames (SCBF) are expected to withstand significant
inelastic deformations when subjected to the forces resulting from the motions of the
design earthquake. SCBF shall meet the requirements in this Section.
