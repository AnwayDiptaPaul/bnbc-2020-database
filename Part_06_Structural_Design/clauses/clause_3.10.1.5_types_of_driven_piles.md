# Section 3.10.1.5: Types of driven piles

3.10.1.5 Types of driven piles
Driven piles shall be classified as "friction" or "end bearing" or a combination of
both according to the manner in which load transfer is developed. The ultimate load
capacity of a pile consists of two parts. One part is due to friction called skin friction
or shaft friction or side shear, and the other is due to end bearing at the base or tip of
the pile. If the skin friction is greater than about 80% of the end bearing load
capacity, the pile is deemed a friction pile and, if the reverse, an end bearing pile. If
the end bearing is neglected, the pile is called a “floating pile”.
