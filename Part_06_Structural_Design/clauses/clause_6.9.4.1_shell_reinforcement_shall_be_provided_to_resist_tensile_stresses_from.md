# Section 6.9.4.1: Shell reinforcement shall be provided to resist tensile stresses from

6.9.4.1 Shell reinforcement shall be provided to resist tensile stresses from
internal membrane forces, to resist tension from bending and twisting
moments, to limit shrinkage and temperature crack width and spacing, and as
reinforcement at shell boundaries, load attachments, and shell openings.
