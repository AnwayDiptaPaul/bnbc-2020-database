# Section 3.17.1.1: Waterproofing where hydrostatic pressure occurs

3.17.1.1 Waterproofing where hydrostatic pressure occurs
Where ground water investigation indicates that a hydrostatic pressure condition
exists, or is likely to occur, walls and floors shall be waterproofed in accordance with
the provisions stated as under.
