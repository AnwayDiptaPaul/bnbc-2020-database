# Section 12.4.4.2: Fatigue stress range

12.4.4.2 Fatigue stress range
For ferrocement structures to sustain a minimum fatigue life of two million cycles, the
stress range in the reinforcement shall be limited to 200 N/mm2. A stress range of 350
N/mm2 shall be used for one million cycles. Higher values may be considered if justified
by tests.
