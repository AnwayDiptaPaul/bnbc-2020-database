# Section 8.2.18.4: Where any plain wires, or deformed wires larger than ASTM D 31, are

8.2.18.4 Where any plain wires, or deformed wires larger than ASTM D 31, are
present in the welded deformed wire reinforcement in the direction of the
development length, the reinforcement shall be developed in accordance with
Sec 8.2.19.
