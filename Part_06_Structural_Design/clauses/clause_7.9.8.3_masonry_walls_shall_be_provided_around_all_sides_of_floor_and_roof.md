# Section 7.9.8.3: Masonry walls shall be provided around all sides of floor and roof

7.9.8.3 Masonry walls shall be provided around all sides of floor and roof
systems in accordance with Figure 6.7.5. The cumulative length of exterior
masonry walls along each side of the floor or roof systems shall be at least 20
percent of the parallel dimension. Required elements shall be without openings
and shall not be less that 1.25 m in width.
Interior cross walls at right angles to bearing walls shall be provided when the
length of the building perpendicular to the span of the floor of roof framing
exceeds twice the distance between shear walls or 10 m, whichever is greater.
