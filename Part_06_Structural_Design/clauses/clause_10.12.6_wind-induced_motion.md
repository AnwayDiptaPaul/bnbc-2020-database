# Section 10.12.6: Wind-Induced Motion

10.12.6 Wind-Induced Motion
The effect of wind-induced motion of buildings on the comfort of occupants shall be
considered. For flexible building and structures as defined in Sec. 2.4.2 Chapter 2, it must
be shown by a rational dynamic analysis that wind induced vibration does not cause any
discomfort to occupants as well as the wind induced dynamic effect does not cause any
adverse effect on the safety, stability and durability of the structure.
