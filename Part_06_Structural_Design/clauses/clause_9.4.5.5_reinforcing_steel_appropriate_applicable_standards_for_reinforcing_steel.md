# Section 9.4.5.5: Reinforcing steel: Appropriate applicable standards for reinforcing steel

9.4.5.5 Reinforcing steel: Appropriate applicable standards for reinforcing steel
are given in Chapter 5 and also listed in Table 6.9.10. Unless specifically
applicable to prestressed concrete, general design requirements of reinforcing
steel are those that has been laid down in Chapter 6 of the Code.
