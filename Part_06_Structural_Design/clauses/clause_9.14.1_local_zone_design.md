# Section 9.14.1: Local Zone Design

9.14.1 Local Zone Design
Monostrand or single 16 mm diameter or smaller diameter bar anchorage
devices and local zone reinforcement shall meet the requirements of ACI 423.7
or the special anchorage device requirements of Sec 9.15.2.
