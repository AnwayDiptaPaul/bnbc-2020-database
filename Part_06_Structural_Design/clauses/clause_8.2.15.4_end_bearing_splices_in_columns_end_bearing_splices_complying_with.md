# Section 8.2.15.4: End bearing splices in columns:  End bearing splices complying with

8.2.15.4 End bearing splices in columns:  End bearing splices complying with
Sec 8.2.14.4 may be used for column bars stressed in compression provided the
splices are staggered or additional bars are provided at splice locations. The
continuing bars in each face of the column shall have a tensile strength at least
0.25 times the area of the vertical reinforcement in that face.
