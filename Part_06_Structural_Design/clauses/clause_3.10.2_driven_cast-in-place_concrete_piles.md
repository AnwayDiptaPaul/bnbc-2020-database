# Section 3.10.2: Driven Cast-in-Place Concrete Piles

3.10.2 Driven Cast-in-Place Concrete Piles
Driven cast-in-place concrete piles shall be in general cast in metal shells driven into
the soil that will remain permanently in place. However, other types of cast-in-place
piles, plain or reinforced, cased or uncased, may be used if the soil conditions permit
their use and if their design and method of placing are satisfactory.
