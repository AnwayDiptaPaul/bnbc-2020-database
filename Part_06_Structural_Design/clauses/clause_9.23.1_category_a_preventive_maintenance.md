# Section 9.23.1: Category A : Preventive Maintenance

9.23.1 Category A : Preventive Maintenance
Maintenance to prevent deterioration which would otherwise lead to
unsatisfactory structural performance. Category A structures are those

for which remedial actions are difficult to take after deterioration
becomes apparent;

of which deterioration must not be apparent;

having a long design life.
Structures in this category generally have a high degree of importance which in
many cases require monitoring.
