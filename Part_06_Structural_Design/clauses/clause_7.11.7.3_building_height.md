# Section 7.11.7.3: Building Height

7.11.7.3 Building Height
Confined masonry is suitable for low- to medium-rise building construction.
Confined masonry buildings will be subject to the following height restrictions:
(a) Up to 4-storey high for Seismic Zone 1 and 2
(b) Up to 3-storey high for Seismic Zone 3
(c) Up to 2-storey high for Seismic Zone 4
