# Section 9.6.10: The effect of reverse friction shall be taken into consideration in such

9.6.10 The effect of reverse friction shall be taken into consideration in such
cases where the initial tension applied to a prestressing tendon is partially
released (e.g., anchorage slip) and action of friction in the reverse direction
causes significant alteration in the distribution of stress along the length of the
tendon.
