# Section 6.5.8.2: Scope and limitations

6.5.8.2 Scope and limitations
Chapter 6
6.5.8.2.1
The provisions of this section may be used as alternative to those of
Sections 6.5.1 to 6.5.7 for two-way slabs supported on all four edges by walls, steel
beams or monolithic concrete beams having a total depth not less than 3 times the
slab thickness.
6.5.8.2.2
Panels shall be rectangular with a longer to shorter centre to centre
support span ratio of not greater than 2.
