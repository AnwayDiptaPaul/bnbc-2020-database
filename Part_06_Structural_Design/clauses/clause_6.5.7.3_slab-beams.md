# Section 6.5.7.3: Slab-beams

6.5.7.3 Slab-beams
6.5.7.3.1
Determination of the moment of inertia of slab-beams at any cross
section outside of joints or column capitals using the gross area of concrete shall be
permitted.
6.5.7.3.2
Variation in moment of inertia along axis of slab-beams shall be taken
into account.
6.5.7.3.3

column, bracket, or capital shall be assumed equal to the moment of inertia of the
