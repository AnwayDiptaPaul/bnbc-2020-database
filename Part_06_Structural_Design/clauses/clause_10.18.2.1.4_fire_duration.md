# Section 10.18.2.1.4: Fire duration

10.18.2.1.4 Fire duration
The fire duration in a particular area shall be determined by considering the total
combustible mass, in other words, fuel load available in the space. In the case of either a
localized fire or a post-flashover compartment fire, the time duration shall be determined
as the total combustible mass divided by the mass loss rate, except where determined from
Sec 10.18.2.1.2.
