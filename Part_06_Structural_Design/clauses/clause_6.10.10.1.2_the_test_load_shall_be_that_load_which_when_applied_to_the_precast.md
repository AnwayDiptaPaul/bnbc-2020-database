# Section 6.10.10.1.2: The test load shall be that load which, when applied to the precast

6.10.10.1.2 The test load shall be that load which, when applied to the precast
member alone, induces the same total force in the tension reinforcement as
would be induced by loading the composite member with the test load required
by Sec 6.11.3.2.
