# Section 4.7.1.3: Fixing methods and fastening devices

4.7.1.3 Fixing methods and fastening devices
In case of butt joints the tie maybe passed through a pre-drilled hole or around
hardwood or bamboo pegs or dowels inserted into prefomed holes to act as horns.
Pegs are driven from one side, usually at an angle to increase strength and dowels
pass right through the member, usually at right angles.
