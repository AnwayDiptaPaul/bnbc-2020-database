# Section 6.1.14.6.2: Reinforcement normal to the ribs shall be provided in the slab as

6.1.14.6.2 Reinforcement normal to the ribs shall be provided in the slab as
required for flexure, considering load concentrations, if any, but not less than
required by Sec 8.1.11
