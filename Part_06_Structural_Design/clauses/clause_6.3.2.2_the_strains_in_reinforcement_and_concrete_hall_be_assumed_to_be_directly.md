# Section 6.3.2.2: The strains in reinforcement and concrete hall be assumed to be directly

6.3.2.2 The strains in reinforcement and concrete hall be assumed to be directly
proportional to the distance from the neutral axis, except that, for deep beams as
defined in Sec 6.3.7.1, an analysis that considers a nonlinear distribution of strain
shall be used. Alternatively, it shall be permitted to use a strut-and-tie model.
See Sections 6.3.7, 6.4.6, and Appendix I.
