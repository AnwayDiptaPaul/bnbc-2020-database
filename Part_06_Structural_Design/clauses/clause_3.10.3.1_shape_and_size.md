# Section 3.10.3.1: Shape and size

3.10.3.1 Shape and size
Prestressed concrete piles that are generally octagonal, square or circular shall be of
approved size and shape. Concrete in prestressed piles shall have a minimum
compressive strength (cylinder), B+′ of 35 MPa at 28 days. Prestressed concrete piles
may be solid or hollow. For hollow piles, precautionary measures should be taken to
prevent breakage due to internal water pressure during driving.
