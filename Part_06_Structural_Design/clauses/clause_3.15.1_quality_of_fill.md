# Section 3.15.1: Quality of Fill

3.15.1 Quality of Fill
The excavation outside the foundation shall be backfilled with soil that is free of
organic material, construction debris and large rocks. The backfill shall be placed in
lifts and compacted in a manner which does not damage foundation, the
waterproofing or damp-proofing material.
