# Section 6.9.4.3: The area of shell reinforcement at any section as measured in two

6.9.4.3 The area of shell reinforcement at any section as measured in two
orthogonal directions shall not be less than the slab shrinkage or temperature
reinforcement required by Sec 8.1.11.
