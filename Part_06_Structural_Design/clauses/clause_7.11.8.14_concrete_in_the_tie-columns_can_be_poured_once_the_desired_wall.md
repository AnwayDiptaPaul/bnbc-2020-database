# Section 7.11.8.14: Concrete in the tie-columns can be poured once the desired wall

7.11.8.14 Concrete in the tie-columns can be poured once the desired wall
height has been reached. The masonry walls provide formwork for the tie-
columns on two sides; however the formwork must be placed on the remaining
two sides.
