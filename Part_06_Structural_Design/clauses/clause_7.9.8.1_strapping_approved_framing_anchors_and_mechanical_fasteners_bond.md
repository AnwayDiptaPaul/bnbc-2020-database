# Section 7.9.8.1: Strapping, approved framing anchors and mechanical fasteners, bond

7.9.8.1 Strapping, approved framing anchors and mechanical fasteners, bond
beams and vertical reinforcement shall be installed to provide a continuous tie
from the roof to foundation system as shown in Figure 6.7.4. In addition, roof
and floor systems, masonry shear walls, or masonry or wood cross walls shall
be provided for lateral stability.
