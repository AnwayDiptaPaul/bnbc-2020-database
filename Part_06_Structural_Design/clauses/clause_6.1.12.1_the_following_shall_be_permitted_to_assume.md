# Section 6.1.12.1: The following shall be permitted to assume:

6.1.12.1 The following shall be permitted to assume:
(a) The live load is applied only to the floor or roof under consideration;
and
(b) The far ends of columns built integrally with the structure are
considered to be fixed.
