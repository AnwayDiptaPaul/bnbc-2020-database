# Section 9.20.6: Protection for Prestressing Steel During Welding

9.20.6 Protection for Prestressing Steel During Welding
Burning or welding operations in the vicinity of prestressing steel shall be
performed so that prestressing steel is not subject to excessive temperatures,
welding sparks, or ground currents.
