# Section 6.6.3.2: Minimum ratio of vertical reinforcement area to gross concrete area, e,

6.6.3.2 Minimum ratio of vertical reinforcement area to gross concrete area, e,
shall be:
(a) 0.0012 for deformed bars not larger than 16 mm diameter with   not
less than 420 MPa; or
(b) 0.0015 for other deformed bars; or
(c) 0.0012 for welded wire reinforcement not larger than MW200 or
MD200.
