# Section 8.2.15.3: Welded splices or mechanical connectors in columns: Welded splices

8.2.15.3 Welded splices or mechanical connectors in columns: Welded splices
or mechanical connectors in columns shall meet the requirements of Sec
8.2.12.3(c) or Sec 8.2.12.3(d).
