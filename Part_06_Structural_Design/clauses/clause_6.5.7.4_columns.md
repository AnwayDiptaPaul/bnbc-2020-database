# Section 6.5.7.4: Columns

6.5.7.4 Columns
6.5.7.4.1
Determination of the moment of inertia of columns at any cross section
outside of joints or column capitals using the gross area of concrete shall be
permitted.
6.5.7.4.2
Variation in moment of inertia along axis of columns shall be taken into
account (Figure 6.6.23).
6.5.7.4.3
Moment of inertia of columns from top to bottom of the slab-beam at a
joint shall be assumed to be infinite.
