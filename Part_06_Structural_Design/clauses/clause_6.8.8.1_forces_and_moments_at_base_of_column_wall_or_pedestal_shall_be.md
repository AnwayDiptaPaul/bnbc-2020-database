# Section 6.8.8.1: Forces and moments at base of column, wall, or pedestal shall be

6.8.8.1 Forces and moments at base of column, wall, or pedestal shall be
transferred to supporting pedestal or footing by bearing on concrete and by
reinforcement, dowels, and mechanical connectors.
6.8.8.1.1
Bearing stress on concrete at contact surface between supported
and supporting member shall not exceed concrete bearing strength for either
surface as given by Sec 6.3.14.
6.8.8.1.2
Reinforcement, dowels, or mechanical connectors between
supported and supporting members shall be adequate to transfer:
(a) All compressive force that exceeds concrete bearing strength of
either member;
(b) Any computed tensile force across interface.
In addition, reinforcement, dowels, or mechanical connectors shall satisfy
Sec 6.8.8.2 or Sec 6.8.8.3.
6.8.8.1.3
If calculated moments are transferred to supporting pedestal or
footing, then reinforcement, dowels, or mechanical connectors shall be
adequate to satisfy Sec 8.2.15.
6.8.8.1.4
Lateral forces shall be transferred to supporting pedestal or footing
in accordance with shear-friction provisions of Sec 6.4.5, or by other
appropriate means.
