# Section 4.7.1.3.3: Pin And Wire Bound Joints

4.7.1.3.3 Pin And Wire Bound Joints
Generally 12 mm diameter bamboo pins are fastened to culms and bound by 2.00
mm diameter galvanized iron wire.
