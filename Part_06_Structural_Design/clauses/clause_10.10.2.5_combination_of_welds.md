# Section 10.10.2.5: Combination of welds

10.10.2.5 Combination of welds
If two or more of the general types of welds (groove, fillet, plug, slot) are combined in a
single joint, the strength of each shall be separately computed with reference to the axis of
the group in order to determine the strength of the combination.
