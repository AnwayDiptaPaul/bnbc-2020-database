# Section 6.10.7: Items Embedded after Concrete Placement

6.10.7 Items Embedded after Concrete Placement
6.10.7.1
When approved by the designer, embedded items (such as dowels or
inserts) that either protrude from the concrete or remain exposed for inspection
shall be permitted to be embedded while the concrete is in a plastic state
provided that Sections 6.10.7.1.1, 6.10.7.1.2, and 6.10.7.1.3 are met.
