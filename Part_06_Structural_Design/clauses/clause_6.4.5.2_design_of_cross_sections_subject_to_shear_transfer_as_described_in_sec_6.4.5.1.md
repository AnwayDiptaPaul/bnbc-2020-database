# Section 6.4.5.2: Design of cross sections subject to shear transfer as described in Sec 6.4.5.1

6.4.5.2 Design of cross sections subject to shear transfer as described in Sec 6.4.5.1
shall be based on Eq. 6.6.47, where ’h is calculated in accordance with provisions of
Sec 6.4.5.3 or Sec 6.4.5.4.
