# Section 12.4.1.1: The analysis of a ferrocement cross-section subject to either bending, or to

12.4.1.1 The analysis of a ferrocement cross-section subject to either bending, or to
bending and axial load, whether based on strength or working stresses, is similar to the
analysis of a reinforced concrete beam or column having several layers of steel (Figure
6.12.2).
