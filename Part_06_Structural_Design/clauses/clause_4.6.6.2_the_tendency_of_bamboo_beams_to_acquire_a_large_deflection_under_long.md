# Section 4.6.6.2: The tendency of bamboo beams to acquire a large deflection under long

4.6.6.2 The tendency of bamboo beams to acquire a large deflection under long
continuous loadings due to possible plastic flow, if any shall be taken care of.
Permanent load may be doubled for calculation of deflection under sustained load
(including creep) in case of green bamboo having moisture content exceeding
15 percent.
