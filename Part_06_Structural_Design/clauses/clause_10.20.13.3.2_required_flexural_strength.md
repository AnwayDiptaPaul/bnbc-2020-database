# Section 10.20.13.3.2: Required flexural strength

10.20.13.3.2  Required flexural strength
The required flexural strength of bracing connections shall be equal to 1.1J% (LRFD)
or (1.1/1.5)J% (ASD), as appropriate, of the brace about the critical buckling axis.
Exception:
Brace connections that meet the requirements of Sec 10.20.13.3.1 and can accommodate
the inelastic rotations associated with brace post-buckling deformations need not meet
this requirement.
