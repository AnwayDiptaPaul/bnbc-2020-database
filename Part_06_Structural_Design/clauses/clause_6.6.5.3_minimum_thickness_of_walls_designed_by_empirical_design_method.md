# Section 6.6.5.3: Minimum thickness of walls designed by empirical design method

6.6.5.3 Minimum thickness of walls designed by empirical design method
6.6.5.3.1
Thickness of bearing walls shall not be less than 1/25 the
supported height or length, whichever is shorter, nor less than 100 mm.
6.6.5.3.2
Thickness of exterior basement walls and foundation walls shall not
be less than 190 mm.
