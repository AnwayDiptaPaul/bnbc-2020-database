# Section 6.5.6.6: Factored moments in middle strips

6.5.6.6 Factored moments in middle strips
6.5.6.6.1
That portion of negative and positive factored moments not resisted by
column strips shall be proportionately assigned to corresponding half middle strips.
6.5.6.6.2
Each middle strip shall be proportioned to resist the sum of the
moments assigned to its two half middle strips.
6.5.6.6.3
A middle strip adjacent to and parallel with a wall-supported edge shall
be proportioned to resist twice the moment assigned to the half middle strip
corresponding to the first row of interior supports.
