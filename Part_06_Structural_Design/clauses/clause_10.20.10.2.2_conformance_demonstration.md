# Section 10.20.10.2.2: Conformance demonstration

10.20.10.2.2 Conformance demonstration
Conformance demonstration shall be as described in Sec 10.20.9.2.2 to satisfy the
requirements of Sec 10.20.10.2.1 for IMF, except that a connection prequalified for IMF in
accordance with ANSI/AISC 358, or as otherwise determined in a connection
prequalification in accordance with Appendix N, or as determined in a program of
qualification testing in accordance with Appendix Q.
