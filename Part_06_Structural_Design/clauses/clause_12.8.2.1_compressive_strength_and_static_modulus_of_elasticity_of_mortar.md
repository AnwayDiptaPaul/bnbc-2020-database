# Section 12.8.2.1: Compressive strength and static modulus of elasticity of mortar

12.8.2.1 Compressive strength and static modulus of elasticity of mortar
The compressive strength and static modulus of elasticity of the mortar used for the
fabrication of ferrocement shall be determined from 75 mm x 150 mm cylinders tested in
accordance with ASTM C39 and C469, respectively.
