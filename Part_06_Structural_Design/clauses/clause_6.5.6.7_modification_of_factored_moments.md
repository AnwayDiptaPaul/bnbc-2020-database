# Section 6.5.6.7: Modification of factored moments

6.5.6.7 Modification of factored moments
Modification of negative and positive factored moments by 10 percent shall be
permitted provided the total static moment for a panel, po, in the direction
considered is not less than that required by Eq. 6.6.84.
