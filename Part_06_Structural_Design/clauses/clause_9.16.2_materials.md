# Section 9.16.2: Materials

9.16.2 Materials
Basically the materials used in CWPC technology are steel and concrete.
(a) Steel: steel used for CWPC is obtained by cold drawing. Cold drawing as
already mentioned is a process of reducing the diameter of the coil rod
by forcing it to pass through a conical die. By this process, the usable
strength of steel can be increased by nearly 100%.
(b) Concrete: The requirement of concrete in CWPC is same as that of
ordinary reinforced concrete.
