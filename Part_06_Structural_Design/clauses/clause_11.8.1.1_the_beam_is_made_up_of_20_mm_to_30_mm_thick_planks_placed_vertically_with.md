# Section 11.8.1.1: The beam is made up of 20 mm to 30 mm thick planks placed vertically with

11.8.1.1  The beam is made up of 20 mm to 30 mm thick planks placed vertically with
joints staggered in the adjoining planks with a minimum distance of 300 mm. The planks
are laminated with the help of wire nails at regular intervals to take up horizontal shear
developed in the beam besides keeping the planks in position (see Figure 6.11.6).
