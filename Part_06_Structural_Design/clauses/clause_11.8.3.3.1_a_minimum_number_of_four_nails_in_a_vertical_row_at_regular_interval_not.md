# Section 11.8.3.3.1: A minimum number of four nails in a vertical row at regular interval not

11.8.3.3.1 A minimum number of four nails in a vertical row at regular interval not
exceeding 75 mm to take up horizontal shear as well as to keep the planks in position shall
be used. Near the joints of the planks this distance may, however, be limited to 5 cm instead
of 75 mm.
