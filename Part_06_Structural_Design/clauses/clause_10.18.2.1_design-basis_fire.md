# Section 10.18.2.1: Design-basis fire

10.18.2.1  Design-basis fire
A design-basis fire shall be identified to describe the heating conditions for the structure.
These heating conditions shall relate to the fuel commodities and compartment
characteristics present in the assumed fire area. The fuel load density based on the
occupancy of the space shall be considered when determining the total fuel load. Heating
conditions shall be specified either in terms of a heat flux or temperature of the upper gas
layer created by the fire. The variation of the heating conditions with time shall be
determined for the duration of the fire.
When the analysis methods in Sec 10.18.2 are used to demonstrate an equivalency as an
alternative material or method as permitted by Code, the design-basis fire shall be
determined in accordance with ASTM E119.
