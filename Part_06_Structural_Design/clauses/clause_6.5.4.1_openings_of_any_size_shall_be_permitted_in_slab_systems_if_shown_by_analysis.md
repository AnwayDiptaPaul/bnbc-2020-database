# Section 6.5.4.1: Openings of any size shall be permitted in slab systems if shown by analysis

6.5.4.1 Openings of any size shall be permitted in slab systems if shown by analysis
that the design strength is at least equal to the required strength set forth in Sections
6.2.2 and 6.2.3, and that all serviceability conditions, including the limits on
deflections, are met.
