# Section 11.8.3.3.4: If the nails required at a point are more than that can be accommodated in a

11.8.3.3.4  If the nails required at a point are more than that can be accommodated in a
row, then these shall he provided lengthwise of the beam within the distance equal to depth
of beam at standard lengthwise spacing.
