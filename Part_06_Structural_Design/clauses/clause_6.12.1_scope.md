# Section 6.12.1: Scope

6.12.1 Scope
6.12.1.1
Provisions of Sec 6.12 shall apply for design of composite concrete
flexural members defined as precast concrete, cast-in-place concrete elements,
or both, constructed in separate placements but so interconnected that all
elements respond to loads as a unit.
6.12.1.2
All provisions of the Code shall apply to composite concrete flexural
members, except as specifically modified in Sec 6.12.
