# Section 7.11.7.2: Walls

7.11.7.2 Walls
(a) At least two fully confined walls should be provided in each direction.
(b) For Seismic Zone 1 and 2, wall density of at least 2 percent in each of two
orthogonal directions is required to ensure good earthquake performance
of confined masonry construction. The wall density for Seismic Zones 3 and
4 should be at least 4 percent and 5 percent respectively. Wall density can
be defined as the total cross sectional area of all walls in one direction
divided by the sum of the floor plan areas for all floors in a building.
