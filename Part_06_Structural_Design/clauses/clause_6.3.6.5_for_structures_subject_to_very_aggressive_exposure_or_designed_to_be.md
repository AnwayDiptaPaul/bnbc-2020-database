# Section 6.3.6.5: For structures subject to very aggressive exposure or designed to be

6.3.6.5 For structures subject to very aggressive exposure or designed to be
watertight, provisions of Sec 6.3.6.4 are not sufficient. For such structures, special
investigations and precautions are required.
