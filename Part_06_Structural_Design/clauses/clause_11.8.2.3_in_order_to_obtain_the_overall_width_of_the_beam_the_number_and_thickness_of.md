# Section 11.8.2.3: In order to obtain the overall width of the beam, the number and thickness of

11.8.2.3  In order to obtain the overall width of the beam, the number and thickness of
planks to form vertical nailed laminated beams, and also type and size of wire nail shall be
as mentioned in Table 6.11.15. The protruding portion of the nail shall be cut off or
clenched across the grains.
