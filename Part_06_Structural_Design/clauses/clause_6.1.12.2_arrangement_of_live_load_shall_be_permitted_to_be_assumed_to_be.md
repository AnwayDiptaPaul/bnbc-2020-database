# Section 6.1.12.2: Arrangement of live load shall be permitted to be assumed to be

6.1.12.2 Arrangement of live load shall be permitted to be assumed to be
limited to combinations of:
(a) Factored dead load on all spans with full factored live load on two
adjacent spans; and
(b) Factored dead load on all spans with full factored live load on alternate
spans.
