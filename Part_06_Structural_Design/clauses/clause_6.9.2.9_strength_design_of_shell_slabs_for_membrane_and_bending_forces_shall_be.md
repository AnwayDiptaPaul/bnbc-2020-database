# Section 6.9.2.9: Strength design of shell slabs for membrane and bending forces shall be

6.9.2.9 Strength design of shell slabs for membrane and bending forces shall be
based on the distribution of stresses and strains as determined from either an
elastic or an inelastic analysis.
