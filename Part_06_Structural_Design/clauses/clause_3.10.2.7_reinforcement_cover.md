# Section 3.10.2.7: Reinforcement cover

3.10.2.7 Reinforcement cover
The reinforcement shall be placed a clear distance of not less than 50 mm from the
cased or uncased sides. When piles are in corrosive or marine environments, or when
concrete is placed by the water or slurry displacement methods, the clear distance
shall not be less than 75 mm for uncased piles and piles with shells not sufficiently
corrosion resistant. Reinforcements shall extend to within 100 mm of the edge of the
pile cap.
