# Section 6.9.1.7: Elastic analysis

6.9.1.7 Elastic analysis
An analysis of deformations and internal forces based on equilibrium,
compatibility of strains, and assumed elastic behavior, and representing to a
suitable approximation the three-dimensional action of the shell together with
its auxiliary members.
