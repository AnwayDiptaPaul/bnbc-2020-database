# Section 11.6.7.1: Solid columns

11.6.7.1 Solid columns
Solid columns shall be classified into short, intermediate and long columns depending upon
their slenderness ratio S d
⁄  as follows:
(a) Short columns - where S d
⁄  does not exceed 11.
(b) Intermediate columns - where S d
⁄  is between 11 and Kb, and
(c) Long columns - where S d
⁄  is greater than Kb.
