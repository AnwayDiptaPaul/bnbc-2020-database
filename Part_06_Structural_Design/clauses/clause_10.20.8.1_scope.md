# Section 10.20.8.1: Scope

10.20.8.1 Scope
Members in the seismic load resisting system (SLRS) shall comply with the specifications
of Sections 10.1 to 10.11 and Sec 10.20.8. For columns that are not part of the SLRS, see
Sec 10.20.8.4.2.
