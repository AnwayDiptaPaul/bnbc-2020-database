# Section 12.7.1.1: Delaminations

12.7.1.1 Delaminations
Delaminations occur when ferrocement splits between layers in laminated constructions
due to springing back or bridging of the mesh during construction. Delamination
sometimes occurs at or near the neutral axis under impact or flexure when there are many
voids in the interior layers. Such areas give off a hollow sound when tapped with a hammer
or stroked with a steel bar.
