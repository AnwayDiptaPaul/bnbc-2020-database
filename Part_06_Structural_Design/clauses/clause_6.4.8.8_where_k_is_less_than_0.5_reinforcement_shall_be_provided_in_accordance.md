# Section 6.4.8.8: Where ’k is less than 0.5•’, reinforcement shall be provided in accordance

6.4.8.8 Where ’k is less than 0.5•’, reinforcement shall be provided in accordance
with Sec 6.4.8.9 or in accordance with Sec. 6.6. Where ’k exceeds 0.5•’, wall
reinforcement for resisting shear shall be provided in accordance with Sec 6.4.8.9.
