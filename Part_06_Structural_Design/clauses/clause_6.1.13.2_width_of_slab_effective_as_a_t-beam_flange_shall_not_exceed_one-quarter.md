# Section 6.1.13.2: Width of slab effective as a T-beam flange shall not exceed one-quarter

6.1.13.2 Width of slab effective as a T-beam flange shall not exceed one-quarter
of the span length of the beam, and the effective overhanging flange width on
each side of the web shall not exceed:
(a) Eight times the slab thickness; and
(b) One-half the clear distance to the next web.
