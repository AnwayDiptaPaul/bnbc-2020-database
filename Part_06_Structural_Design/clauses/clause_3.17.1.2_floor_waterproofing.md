# Section 3.17.1.2: Floor waterproofing

3.17.1.2 Floor waterproofing
Floors required to be waterproofed shall be of concrete and shall be designed and
constructed to withstand the anticipated hydrostatic pressure. Waterproofing of the
floor shall be accomplished by placing under the slab a membrane of rubberized
asphalt, or butyl rubber, or polymer modified asphalt, or neoprene, or not less than
0.15 mm polyvinyl chloride or polyethylene, or other approved materials, capable of
bridging nonstructural cracks. Joints in the membrane shall be lapped not less than
150 mm and sealed in an approved manner.
