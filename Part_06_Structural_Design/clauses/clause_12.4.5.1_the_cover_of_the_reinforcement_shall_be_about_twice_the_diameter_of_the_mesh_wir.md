# Section 12.4.5.1: The cover of the reinforcement shall be about twice the diameter of the mesh wire

12.4.5.1 The cover of the reinforcement shall be about twice the diameter of the mesh wire
or thickness of other reinforcement used. A smaller cover is acceptable provided the
reinforcement is not susceptible to rapid corrosion, the surface is protected by an
appropriate coating, and the crack width is limited to 0.05 mm. For ferrocement elements
of thickness less than 25 mm, a cover of the order of 2 mm shall be provided.
