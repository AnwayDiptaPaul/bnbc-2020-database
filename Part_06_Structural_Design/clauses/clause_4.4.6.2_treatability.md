# Section 4.4.6.2: Treatability

4.4.6.2 Treatability
Due to difference in the anatomical structure of bamboo as compared to timber,
bamboo behaves entirely differently from wood during treatment with preservative.
Bamboos are difficult to treat by normal preservation methods in dry condition and
therefore treatment is best carried out in green condition.
