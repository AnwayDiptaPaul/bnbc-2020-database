# Section 6.6.7.1: Walls designed as grade beams shall have top and bottom

6.6.7.1 Walls designed as grade beams shall have top and bottom
reinforcement as required for moment in accordance with provisions of
Sections 6.3.2 to 6.3.7. Design for shear shall be in accordance with provisions of
Sec. 6.4.
