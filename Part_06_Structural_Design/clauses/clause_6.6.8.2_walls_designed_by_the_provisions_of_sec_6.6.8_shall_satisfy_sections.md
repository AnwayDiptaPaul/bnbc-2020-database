# Section 6.6.8.2: Walls designed by the provisions of Sec 6.6.8 shall satisfy Sections

6.6.8.2 Walls designed by the provisions of Sec 6.6.8 shall satisfy Sections
6.6.8.2.1 to 6.6.8.2.6.
6.6.8.2.1
The wall panel shall be designed as a simply supported, axially
loaded member subjected to an out-of-plane uniform lateral load, with
maximum moments and deflections occurring at midspan.
6.6.8.2.2
The cross section shall be constant over the height of the panel.
6.6.8.2.3
The wall shall be tension-controlled.
