# Section 10.20.7.3: Welded Joints

10.20.7.3 Welded Joints
Welding shall be performed in accordance with Appendix S. Welding shall be performed
in accordance with a welding procedure specification (WPS) as required in AWS D1.1.
The WPS variables shall be within the parameters established by the filler metal
manufacturer.
