# Section 9.20.5: Mixing and Pumping of Grout

9.20.5 Mixing and Pumping of Grout
9.20.5.1
Grout shall be mixed in equipment capable of continuous mechanical
mixing and agitation that will produce uniform distribution of materials, passed
through screens, and pumped in a manner that will completely fill the ducts.
9.20.5.2
Temperature of members at time of grouting shall be above 2°C and
shall be maintained above 2°C until field-cured 50 mm cubes of grout reach a
minimum compressive strength of 5.5 N/mm2.
9.20.5.3
Grout temperatures shall not be above 32°C during mixing and
pumping.
