# Section 4.6.5.1: Main structural components in bamboo may include roof and floor

4.6.5.1 Main structural components in bamboo may include roof and floor
diaphragms, shear walls, wall panellings, beams, piles, columns, etc. Both from the
point of view of capacity and deformation, trusses and framed skeletons are much
better applications of bamboo.
