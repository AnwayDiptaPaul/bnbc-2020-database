# Section 9.4.5.6: Modulus of elasticity, : Where it is not possible to ascertain the

9.4.5.6 Modulus of elasticity, : Where it is not possible to ascertain the
modulus of elasticity of reinforcing steel by test and from the manufacturer of
steel, the modulus of elasticity of reinforcing steel may be permitted to be taken
as  = 200,000 N/mm2.
