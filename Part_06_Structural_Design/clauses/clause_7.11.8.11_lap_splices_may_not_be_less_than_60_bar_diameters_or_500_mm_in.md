# Section 7.11.8.11: Lap splices may not be less than 60 bar diameters or 500 mm in

7.11.8.11 Lap splices may not be less than 60 bar diameters or 500 mm in
length. Splicing should take place at column mid height, except for the ground
floor level (where splicing is not permitted).
