# Section 3.19.1: Footings on Filled up Ground

3.19.1 Footings on Filled up Ground
Footings shall not be constructed on loosely filled up ground with non-uniform
density or consistency, unless adequate strengthening of the soil is made by applying
ground improvement techniques.
