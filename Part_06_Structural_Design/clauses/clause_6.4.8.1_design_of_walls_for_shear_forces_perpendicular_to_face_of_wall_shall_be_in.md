# Section 6.4.8.1: Design of walls for shear forces perpendicular to face of wall shall be in

6.4.8.1 Design of walls for shear forces perpendicular to face of wall shall be in
accordance with provisions for slabs in Sec 6.4.10. Design for horizontal in-plane
shear forces in a wall shall be in accordance with Sections 6.4.8.2 to 6.4.8.9.
Alternatively, it shall be permitted to design walls with a height not exceeding two
times the length of the wall for horizontal shear forces in accordance with Appendix I
and Sections 6.4.8.9.2 to 6.4.8.9.5.
