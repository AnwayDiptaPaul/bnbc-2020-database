# Section 10.20.8.5: Column bases

10.20.8.5 Column bases
The required strength of column bases shall be calculated in accordance with Sections
10.20.8.5.1, 10.20.8.5.2, and 10.20.8.5.3. The available strength of anchor rods
shall be determined in accordance with Specification Sec 10.10.3.
The available strength of concrete elements at the column base, including anchor rod
embedment and reinforcing steel, shall be in accordance with Appendix D Chapter 6
Part 6 of this Code.
Exception:
The special requirements in Appendix D Chapter 6 Part 6, for “regions of moderate or
high seismic risk, or for structures assigned to intermediate or high seismic performance
or design categories” need not be applied.
