# Section 4.4.4.1.2: The minimum length of culms shall be preferably 6 m for facilitating close

4.4.4.1.2 The minimum length of culms shall be preferably 6 m for facilitating close
fittings at joints.
4.4.5
Taper
The taper shall not be more than 5.8 mm per metre length (or 0.58 percent) of bamboo in
any grade of bamboo.
