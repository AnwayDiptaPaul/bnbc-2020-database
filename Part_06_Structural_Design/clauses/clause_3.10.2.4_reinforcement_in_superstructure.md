# Section 3.10.2.4: Reinforcement in superstructure

3.10.2.4 Reinforcement in superstructure
Sufficient reinforcement shall be provided at the junction of the pile with the
superstructure to make a suitable connection. The embedment of the reinforcement
into the cap shall be as specified for precast piles.
