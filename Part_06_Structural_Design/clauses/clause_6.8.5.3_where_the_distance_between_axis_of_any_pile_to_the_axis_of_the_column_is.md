# Section 6.8.5.3: Where the distance between axis of any pile to the axis of the column is

6.8.5.3 Where the distance between axis of any pile to the axis of the column is
more than two times the distance between the top of the pile cap and the top of
the pile, the pile cap shall satisfy Sections 6.4.10 and 6.8.5.4. Other pile caps shall
satisfy either Appendix I, or both Sections 6.4.10 and 6.8.5.4. If Appendix I is
used, the effective concrete compression strength of the struts, !, shall be
determined using Sec I.3.2.2(b).
