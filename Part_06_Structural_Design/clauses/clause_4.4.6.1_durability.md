# Section 4.4.6.1: Durability

4.4.6.1 Durability
The natural durability of bamboo is low and varies between 12 months and 36
months depending on the species and climatic conditions. In tropical countries the
bio-deterioration is very severe, Bamboos are generally destroyed in about one to
two years’ time when used in the open and in contact with ground while a service life
of two to five years can be expected from bamboo when used under cover and out of
contact with ground. The mechanical strength of bamboo deteriorates rapidly with
the onset of fungal decay in the sclerenchymatous fibres. Split bamboo is more
rapidly destroyed than round bamboo. For making bamboo durable, suitable
treatment shall be given.
