# Section 11.12.1.2: Permissible defects

11.12.1.2 Permissible defects
(a) Jointing gaps:  Not more than 3 mm wide, provided they are well staggered in their
spacing and position between the successive plies.
(b) Slope of grain:  Not exceeding 1 in 10 in the face layers.
(c) Tight knot:
Three numbers up to 25 mm diameter in one square metre provided
they are spaced 300 mm or more apart.
(d) Warp:
Not exceeding 1.5 mm per metre length.
