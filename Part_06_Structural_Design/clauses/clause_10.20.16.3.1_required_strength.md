# Section 10.20.16.3.1: Required strength

10.20.16.3.1  Required strength
The required strength of bracing connections in tension and compression (including beam-
to-column connections if part of the bracing system) shall be 1.1 times the adjusted brace
strength in compression (LRFD) or (1.1/1.5) times the adjusted brace strength in
compression (ASD).
