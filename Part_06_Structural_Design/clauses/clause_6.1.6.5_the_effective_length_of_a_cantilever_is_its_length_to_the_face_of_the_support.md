# Section 6.1.6.5: The effective length of a cantilever is its length to the face of the support

6.1.6.5 The effective length of a cantilever is its length to the face of the support
plus half its effective depth, except where it forms the end of a continuous beam,
where the length to the centre of support shall be used.
