# Section 6.7.1.5: Design

6.7.1.5 Design
6.7.1.5.1
Strength, deflection and crack control
The recommendations given in Sections 6.1 and 6.2 for beams and one-way
slabs shall apply, except for the span/depth ratio of staircases without stringer
beam where the provision of Sec 6.7.1.5.2 below shall apply.
6.7.1.5.2
Permissible span/effective depth ratio for staircase without
stringer beams: In case of stair flight that occupies at least 60% of the span, the
ratio calculated in accordance with Sec 6.2.5.2 shall be increased by 15%.
6.7.2
Special Types of Stairs
The provisions of special types of stairs like Free Standing (Landing
unsupported), Sawtooth (Slabless) and Helicoidal are provided in Appendix M.
