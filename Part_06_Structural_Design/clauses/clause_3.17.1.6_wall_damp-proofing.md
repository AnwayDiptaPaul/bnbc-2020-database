# Section 3.17.1.6: Wall damp-proofing

3.17.1.6 Wall damp-proofing
For walls, damp-proofing materials shall be installed and shall extend from a point
150 mm above grade, down to the top of the spread portion of the foundation.
Wall damp-proofing material shall consist of a bituminous material, acrylic modified
cement base coating, rubberized asphalt, polymer-modified asphalt, butyl rubber, or
other approved materials capable of bridging nonstructural cracks.
