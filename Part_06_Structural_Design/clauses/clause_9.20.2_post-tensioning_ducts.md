# Section 9.20.2: Post-tensioning Ducts

9.20.2 Post-tensioning Ducts
9.20.2.1
Ducts for grouted tendons shall be mortar- tight and nonreactive
with concrete, prestressing steel, grout, and corrosion inhibitor.
9.20.2.2
Ducts for grouted single-wire, single-strand, or single-bar tendons
shall have an inside diameter at least 6 mm larger than the prestressing steel
diameter.
9.20.2.3
Ducts for grouted multiple wire, multiple strand, or multiple bar
tendons shall  have an inside cross-sectional area at least two times the cross-
sectional area of the prestressing steel.
9.20.2.4
Ducts shall be maintained free of ponded water if members to be
grouted are exposed to temperatures below freezing prior to grouting.
