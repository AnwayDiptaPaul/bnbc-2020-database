# Section 10.13.7.4: Inspection of slip-critical high-strength bolted connections

10.13.7.4 Inspection of slip-critical high-strength bolted connections
The inspection of slip-critical high-strength bolted connections shall be in accordance with
the provisions of the RCSC Specification for Structural Joints Using ASTM A325 or A490
Bolts.
