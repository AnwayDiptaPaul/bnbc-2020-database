# Section 7.11.8.10: Special lintel beams may be required across larger openings having a

7.11.8.10 Special lintel beams may be required across larger openings having a
width exceeding 1.5 m. Additional reinforcement bars need to be provided.
Lintel beams can be integrated with the tie-beams at the floor level.
