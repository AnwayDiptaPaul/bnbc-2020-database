# Section 13.2.1.6.1: Longitudinal bars

13.2.1.6.1 Longitudinal bars
The concrete encasement shall be reinforced with longitudinal bars and lateral ties
extending completely around the structural steel core. The clear cover shall not be less than
40 mm.
The longitudinal bars shall
(a) Be continuous at framed levels when considered to carry load;
(b) Have an area not less than 0.01 times the total gross cross-sectional area;
(c) Be located at each corner; and
(d) Spaced on all sides not further apart than 525t/%' times one-half the least
dimension of the composite section.
