# Section 9.4.2.1: Strength design of prestressed members for flexure and axial loads shall

9.4.2.1 Strength design of prestressed members for flexure and axial loads shall
be based on assumptions given in Sections 9.4.2.2 to 9.4.2.7 and shall satisfy the
applicable conditions of equilibrium and compatibility of strains.
