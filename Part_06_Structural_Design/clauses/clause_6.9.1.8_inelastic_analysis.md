# Section 6.9.1.8: Inelastic analysis

6.9.1.8 Inelastic analysis
An analysis of deformations and internal forces based on equilibrium, nonlinear
stress-strain relations for concrete and reinforcement, consideration of cracking
and time-dependent effects, and compatibility of strains. The analysis shall
represent to a suitable approximation three-dimensional action of the shell
together with its auxiliary members.
