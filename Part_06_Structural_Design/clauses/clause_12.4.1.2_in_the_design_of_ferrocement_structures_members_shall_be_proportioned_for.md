# Section 12.4.1.2: In the design of ferrocement structures, members shall be proportioned for

12.4.1.2 In the design of ferrocement structures, members shall be proportioned for
adequate strength as per the provisions of this Chapter using load factors and strength
reduction factors specified in Chapter 6.
