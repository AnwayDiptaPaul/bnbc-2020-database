# Section 3.10.6.18: Negative skin friction

3.10.6.18 Negative skin friction
Evaluation of negative skin friction shall include a load-transfer method of analysis
to determine the neutral point (i.e., point of zero relative displacement) and load
distribution along shaft (e.g., Reese and O'Neill, 1988). Due to the possible time
dependence associated with vertical ground movement, the analysis shall consider
the effect of time on load transfer between the ground and shaft and the analysis shall
be performed for the time period relating to the maximum axial load transfer to the
