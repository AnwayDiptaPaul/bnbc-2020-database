# Section 6.5.9.5: Size and Position of Ribs

6.5.9.5 Size and Position of Ribs
In-situ-ribs shall be not less than 100 mm wide. They shall be spaced at centres not
greater than 750 mm  apart and their depth, excluding any topping, shall be not more
than three and half  times their width. Ribs shall be formed along each edge parallel
to the span of one-way slabs.
