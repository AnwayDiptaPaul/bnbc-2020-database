# Section 12.4.5.6: For a given volume fraction of reinforcement, better performance in terms of

12.4.5.6 For a given volume fraction of reinforcement, better performance in terms of
crack widths, water tightness, and ductility may be achieved by uniform distribution of the
reinforcement throughout the thickness and by increasing its specific surface. A minimum
of two layers of mesh shall be acceptable, but more than two layers of reinforcement are
preferable.
