# Section 6.1.5.1: It shall be permitted to decrease factored moments calculated by elastic

6.1.5.1 It shall be permitted to decrease factored moments calculated by elastic
theory at sections of maximum negative or maximum positive moment in any
span of continuous flexural members for any assumed loading arrangement by
approximate values for moments are used.
greater than 0.0075 at the section at which moment is reduced.
