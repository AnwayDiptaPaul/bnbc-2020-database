# Section 6.8.9.1: In sloped or stepped footings, angle of slope or depth and location of

6.8.9.1 In sloped or stepped footings, angle of slope or depth and location of
steps shall be such that design requirements are satisfied at every section. (See
also Sec 8.2.7.6.)
