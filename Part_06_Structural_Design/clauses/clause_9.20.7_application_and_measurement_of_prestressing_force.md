# Section 9.20.7: Application and Measurement of Prestressing Force

9.20.7 Application and Measurement of Prestressing Force
9.20.7.1
Prestressing force shall be determined by both of (a) and (b):
(a) Measurement of steel elongation. Required elongation shall be
determined from average load-elongation curves for the prestressing
steel used;
(b) Observation of jacking force on a calibrated gage or load cell or by use of
a calibrated dynamometer.
Cause of any difference in force determination between (a) and (b) that exceeds
5 percent for pretensioned elements or 7 percent for post-tensioned
construction shall be ascertained and corrected.
