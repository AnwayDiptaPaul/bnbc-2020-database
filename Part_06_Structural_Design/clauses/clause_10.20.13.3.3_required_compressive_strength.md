# Section 10.20.13.3.3: Required compressive strength

10.20.13.3.3  Required compressive strength
Bracing connections shall be designed for a required compressive strength based on
buckling limit states that is at least equal to 1.1J%H (LRFD) or (1.1/1.5)J%H (ASD),
as appropriate, where H   is the nominal compressive strength of the brace.
