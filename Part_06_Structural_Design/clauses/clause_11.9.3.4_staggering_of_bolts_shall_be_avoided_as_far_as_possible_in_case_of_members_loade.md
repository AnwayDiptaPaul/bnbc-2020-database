# Section 11.9.3.4: Staggering of bolts shall be avoided as far as possible in case of members loaded

11.9.3.4 Staggering of bolts shall be avoided as far as possible in case of members loaded
parallel to grain of wood. For loads acting perpendicular to grain of wood, staggering is
preferable to avoid splitting due to weather effects.
