# Section 6.9.2.1: Elastic behavior shall be an accepted basis for determining internal

6.9.2.1 Elastic behavior shall be an accepted basis for determining internal
forces and displacements of thin shells. This behavior shall be permitted to be
established by computations based on an analysis of the uncracked concrete
structure in which the material is assumed linearly elastic, homogeneous, and
isotropic. Poisson’s ratio of concrete shall be permitted to be taken equal to
zero.
