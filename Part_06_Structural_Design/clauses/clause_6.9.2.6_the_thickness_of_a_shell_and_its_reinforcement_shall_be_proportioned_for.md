# Section 6.9.2.6: The thickness of a shell and its reinforcement shall be proportioned for

6.9.2.6 The thickness of a shell and its reinforcement shall be proportioned for
the required strength and serviceability, using either the strength design
method of Sec 6.1.2.1 or the design method of Sec 6.1.2.2.
