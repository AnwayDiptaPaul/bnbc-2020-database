# Section 10.2.3.10: Design for fire conditions

10.2.3.10 Design for fire conditions
Two methods of design for fire conditions are provided in Sec 10.18, Structural Design for
Fire Conditions: Qualification Testing and Engineering Analysis. Compliance with the fire
protection requirements in Part 4 of this Code shall be required in addition to the
requirements of Sec 10.18.
