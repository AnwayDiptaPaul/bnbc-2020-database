# Section 4.7.1.3.2: Wire Bound Joints

4.7.1.3.2 Wire Bound Joints
Usually galvanized iron 2.00 mm diameter galvanized iron wire is tightened around
the joints by binding the respective pieces together. At least two holes are drilled in
each piece and wire is passed through them for good results.
