# Section 6.3.9.2: Minimum number of longitudinal bars in compression members shall be 4

6.3.9.2 Minimum number of longitudinal bars in compression members shall be 4
for bars within rectangular or circular ties, 3 for bars within triangular ties, and 6 for
bars enclosed by spirals conforming to Sec 6.3.9.3.
