# Section 7.11.4.1: Wall density

7.11.4.1 Wall density
Wall density is believed to be one of the key parameters influencing the seismic
performance of confined masonry buildings. It can be determined as the
transverse area of walls in each principal direction divided by the total floor
area of the building.
