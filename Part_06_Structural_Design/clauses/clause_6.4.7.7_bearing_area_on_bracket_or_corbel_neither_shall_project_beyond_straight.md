# Section 6.4.7.7: Bearing area on bracket or corbel neither shall project beyond straight

6.4.7.7 Bearing area on bracket or corbel neither shall project beyond straight
portion of primary tension reinforcement, nor shall project beyond interior face of
transverse anchor bar (if one is provided).
