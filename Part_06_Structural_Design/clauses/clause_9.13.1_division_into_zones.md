# Section 9.13.1: Division into Zones

9.13.1 Division into Zones
The anchorage zone shall be considered as composed of two zones as described
below and shown in Figure 6.9.1.
(a) The local zone is the rectangular prism (or equivalent rectangular
prism for circular or oval anchorages) of concrete immediately
surrounding the anchorage device and any confining reinforcement;
(b) The general zone is the anchorage zone beyond the local zone.
