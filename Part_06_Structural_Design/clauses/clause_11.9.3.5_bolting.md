# Section 11.9.3.5: Bolting

11.9.3.5 Bolting
The bolt holes shall be bored or drilled perpendicular to the surface involved. Forcible
driving of the bolts shall be avoided which may cause cracking or splitting of members. A
bolt hole of 1.0 mm oversize may be used as a guide for preboring.
