# Section 9.26.1.3: Deterioration factors

9.26.1.3 Deterioration factors
Deterioration factors may be classified into those
(a) external to structures such as temperature, humidity and any other
environmental characteristics; and
(b) internal to the structure such as design parameters and quality control
during construction.
Commentary:
Design factors include the geometry of the members/ segments, crack width
specifications, concrete cover to reinforcing bar and prestressing steel/ducts,
and design strength. Construction factors include material selection, mix
proportions, transportation, placement, and curing methods.
