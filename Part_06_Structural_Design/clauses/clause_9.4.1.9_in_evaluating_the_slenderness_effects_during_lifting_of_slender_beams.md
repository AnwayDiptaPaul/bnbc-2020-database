# Section 9.4.1.9: In evaluating the slenderness effects during lifting of slender beams,

9.4.1.9 In evaluating the slenderness effects during lifting of slender beams,
consideration shall be given to beam geometry, location of lifting points, method
of lifting and tolerances in construction. All beams which are lifted on vertical or
inclined slings shall be checked for lateral stability and lateral moment on
account of tilting of beam. Reference may be made to specialist literature in this
regard.
