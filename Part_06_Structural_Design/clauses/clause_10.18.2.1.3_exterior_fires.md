# Section 10.18.2.1.3: Exterior fires

10.18.2.1.3 Exterior fires
The exposure of exterior structure to flames projecting from windows or other wall
openings as a result of a post-flashover compartment fire shall be considered along with
the radiation from the interior fire through the opening. The shape and length of the flame
projection shall be used along with the distance between the flame and the exterior
steelwork to determine the heat flux to the steel. The method identified in Sec 10.18.2.1.2
shall be used for describing the characteristics of the interior compartment fire.
