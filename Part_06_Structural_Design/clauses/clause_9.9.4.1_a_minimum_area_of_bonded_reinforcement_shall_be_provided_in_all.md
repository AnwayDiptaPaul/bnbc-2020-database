# Section 9.9.4.1: A minimum area of bonded reinforcement shall be provided in all

9.9.4.1 A minimum area of bonded reinforcement shall be provided in all
flexural members with unbonded tendons as required by Sections 9.9.4.2 and
9.9.4.3.
