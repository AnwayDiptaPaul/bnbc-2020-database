# Section 11.6.6.1: Such structural members shall be investigated for the following:

11.6.6.1  Such structural members shall be investigated for the following:
(a) Bending strength,
(b) Maximum horizontal shear,
(c) Stress at the bearings, and
(d) Deflection.
