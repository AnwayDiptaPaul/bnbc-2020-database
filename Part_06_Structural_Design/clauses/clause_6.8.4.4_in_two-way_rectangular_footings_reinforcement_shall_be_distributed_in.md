# Section 6.8.4.4: In two-way rectangular footings, reinforcement shall be distributed in

6.8.4.4 In two-way rectangular footings, reinforcement shall be distributed in
accordance with Sections 6.8.4.4.1 and 6.8.4.4.2.
6.8.4.4.1
Reinforcement in long direction shall be distributed uniformly
across entire width of footing.
6.8.4.4.2
