# Section 23: Requirement of certification of work

23. Requirement of certification of work.Any planning, design, supervision of
construction, repair, maintenance, modification and alteration of buildings, or any other
work regulated by the Code shall be certified by a licensed engineer, architect or planner
for its compliance with the provisions of the Code  as per Tables 2.3.3 and 2.3.4.
