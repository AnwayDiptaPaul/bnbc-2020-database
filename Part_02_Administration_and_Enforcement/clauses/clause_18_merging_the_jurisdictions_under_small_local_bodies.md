# Section 18: Merging the Jurisdictions under small local bodies

18. Merging the Jurisdictions under small local bodies.Small local bodies like
Pourashavas, Upazila, Union Parishad, located outside the larger city municipalities and
having insufficient funds for individually carrying out the task of the Code enforcing
agency may jointly appoint or designate, with the approval of the Authority, a Building
Official who shall have a jurisdiction over the combined area of jurisdiction of the
concerned local bodies.
