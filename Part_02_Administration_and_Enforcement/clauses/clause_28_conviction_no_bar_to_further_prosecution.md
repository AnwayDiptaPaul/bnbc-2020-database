# Section 28: Conviction no bar to further prosecution

28. Conviction no bar to further prosecution.If a person is convicted under the
provisions of this Code for failing to comply with any of its requirements or obligations
such conviction shall not act as a bar for further prosecution for any subsequent failure on
the part of such person to comply.
