# Section 15: Building Construction Committee

15. Building Construction Committee.(1) The Building Official shall exercise
through a Building Construction Committee comprising four members excluding
Building Official.
(2) Building Construction Committee shall consist of one architect, one civil
engineer, one town planner and representative from concerned body.
(3) Building Official shall work as ex-officio member-secretary of the Committee.
