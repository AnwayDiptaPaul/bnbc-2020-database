# Section 3.3.1: As per the provisions laid out in the Building Construction Act the government

3.3.1  As per the provisions laid out in the Building Construction Act the government
may constitute various committees to examine and scrutinize applications mentioned in
Clause 3.2 above and approve or refuse permits thereby.
3.3.2
Each committee will have specific Terms of Reference and Work
Procedure.
