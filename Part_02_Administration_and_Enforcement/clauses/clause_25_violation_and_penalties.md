# Section 25: Violation and penalties

25. Violation and penalties.Any person, firm, corporation or government
department or agency who as owner of the property erects, constructs, enlarges, alters,
repairs, moves, improves, removes, converts, demolishes, equips, uses, occupies or
maintains any building or structure or cause or permit the same to be done in violation of
this Code shall be guilty of an offence and the Authority shall take legal action against
such offenders as prescribed by law.
Explanation.For the purpose of this provisions the term “owner” shall include any
developer who by appointment, contract or lease is responsible for such activities.
