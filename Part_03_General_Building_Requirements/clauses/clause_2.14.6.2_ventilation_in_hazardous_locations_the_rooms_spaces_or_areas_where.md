# Section 2.14.6.2: Ventilation in Hazardous Locations: The rooms, spaces or areas where

2.14.6.2 Ventilation in Hazardous Locations: The rooms, spaces or areas where
explosive, corrosive, combustible, flammable or highly toxic dust, mists, fumes, vapors
or gases are stored or may be emitted due to the processing, use, handling or storage of
materials shall be mechanically ventilated.
