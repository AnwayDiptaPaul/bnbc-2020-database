# Section 4.5.1.3: For Buildings of Occupancy A5, B, C, E1 and E2, photoelectric sensors shall be

4.5.1.3 For Buildings of Occupancy A5, B, C, E1 and E2, photoelectric sensors shall be
connected to luminaires, to enable dimming or switching off lamps that do not require to
be operated, due to the presence of adequate daylight. The photoelectric sensor shall be
located approximately at half (½) the depth of day-lit zone.
