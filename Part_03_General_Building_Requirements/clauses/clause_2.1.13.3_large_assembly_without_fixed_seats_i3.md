# Section 2.1.13.3: Large Assembly without Fixed Seats (I3)

2.1.13.3 Large Assembly without Fixed Seats (I3)
This occupancy type shall include any building or portion thereof for assembly in a
space, in which there are no fixed seats, which may or may not be provided with a
legitimate stage or theatrical accessories, and which has accommodation for 300 or more
persons.
