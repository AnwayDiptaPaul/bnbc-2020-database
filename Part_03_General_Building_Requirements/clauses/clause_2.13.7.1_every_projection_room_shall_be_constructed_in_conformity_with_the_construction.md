# Section 2.13.7.1: Every projection room shall be constructed in conformity with the construction

2.13.7.1 Every projection room shall be constructed in conformity with the construction
requirements for the type of the building in which the projection room is located. The
wall opening required for projection need not have a fire protection assembly but shall be
closed with glass or other approved materials.
