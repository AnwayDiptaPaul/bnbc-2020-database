# Section 2.14.6.5: Other requirements of water closets are specified in Sec 1.12.4 Chapter 1,

2.14.6.5 Other requirements of water closets are specified in Sec 1.12.4 Chapter 1,
Part 3.
Chapter 2
2.14.7
Sanitation
All buildings or part of a building classified as Occupancy J shall conform to the
provisions of Sec 1.16 of this Chapter and Part 8 of this Code.
2.14.8
Shaft and Exit Enclosures
Elevator shafts, vent shafts and other vertical openings shall be enclosed conforming to
the provisions of Tables 3.3.1 (a) and (b). Exit requirements shall comply with Chapter 3,
Part 4.
2.14.9
Fire Detection, Alarm, Evacuation and Extinguishment System
All buildings shall conform to regulations set forth in Part 4 of this Code.
