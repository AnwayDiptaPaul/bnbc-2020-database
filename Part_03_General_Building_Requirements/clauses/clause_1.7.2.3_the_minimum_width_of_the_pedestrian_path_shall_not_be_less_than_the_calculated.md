# Section 1.7.2.3: The minimum width of the pedestrian path shall not be less than the calculated

1.7.2.3 The minimum width of the pedestrian path shall not be less than the calculated
width of connected corridor or passage or walking ramp of a building for entry or exit
provided it is not enclosed by adjacent walls on both sides; for pedestrian paths enclosed
by adjacent walls on both sides the minimum width shall be 1.25 m. For public buildings
and places where high pedestrian movement is expected, Table 3.F.1 of Appendix F may
be followed.
