# Section 4.4.4.1: Fifty (50) percent of horizontal exposed roof slabs of Buildings of Occupancy

4.4.4.1 Fifty (50) percent of horizontal exposed roof slabs of Buildings of Occupancy
B, C, D and E, shall have green roofing system, to manage water run-off from roof tops,
to control internal temperatures within the top floors and to reduce the carbon footprint of
the building. This shall not include any covered roof surface, e.g. solar panels, solar
thermal heaters, machinery for mechanical or electrical systems, water tanks, etc. Stair
loft or machine room tops will be exempt from this rule.
(a) The roof slab design shall consider structural support of the green roof system,
with growing medium of minimum 300 mm.
(b) The design will indicate protection from dampness and provide a drainage
