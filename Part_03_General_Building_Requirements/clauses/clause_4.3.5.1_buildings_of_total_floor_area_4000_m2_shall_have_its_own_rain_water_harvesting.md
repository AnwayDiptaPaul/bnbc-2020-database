# Section 4.3.5.1: Buildings of total floor area > 4000 m2 shall have its own rain water harvesting

4.3.5.1 Buildings of total floor area > 4000 m2 shall have its own rain water harvesting
system as discussed in Chapter 7 Part 8 and installed complying with Section 7.13 Part 8,
of this Code. The reservoir capacity shall be a multiple of the area of Ground Coverage of
the building and a rain collection coefficient of 0.073.
