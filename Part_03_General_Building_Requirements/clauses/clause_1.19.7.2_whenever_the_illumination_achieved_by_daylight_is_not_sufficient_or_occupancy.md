# Section 1.19.7.2: Whenever the illumination achieved by daylight is not sufficient or occupancy

1.19.7.2 Whenever the illumination achieved by daylight is not sufficient or occupancy
at night is necessary, artificial lighting shall be installed to supplement daylight, or to
provide the required night lighting, in accordance with the provisions of Chapter 1 of
Part 8.
1.19.8
Protected openings, when and where are installed shall not be normally operable
form the inside of a building. Such openings however, shall not be credited towards
meeting any ventilation requirements.
1.19.9
The requirements of opening areas specified in Sec 1.19.6 shall suffice for
ventilation provided that the windows or ventilators forming the opening are operable.
When part of a window area is made of fixed glazing, only the operable portion shall be
counted in aggregating the opening area.
