# Section 2.14.6.3: The mechanical ventilation of all hazardous uses shall be segregated or

2.14.6.3 The mechanical ventilation of all hazardous uses shall be segregated or
separated from the ventilation of other areas. The emissions generated at work areas shall
be confined to the area in which they are generated and shall be removed or discharged
outside the building and preventive measures against back flow of such hazardous fumes
or gases inside the building shall be installed.
