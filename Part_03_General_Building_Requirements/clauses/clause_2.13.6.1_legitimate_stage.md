# Section 2.13.6.1: Legitimate Stage

2.13.6.1 Legitimate Stage
Legitimate stage shall be constructed as specified in Part 4, specifying the type of
construction but shall not be less than construction Type I-C. The position of the
legitimate stage extending beyond the proscenium opening line shall be permitted to be
constructed with two hour fire-resistive materials. The floor of the stage may be
constructed with one hour fire rating materials. Thickness of a wooden floor shall not be
less than 50 mm.
