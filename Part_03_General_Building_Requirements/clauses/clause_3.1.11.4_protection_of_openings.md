# Section 3.1.11.4: Protection of openings

3.1.11.4 Protection of openings
Openings in shaft enclosures shall be protected with a self-closing or an automatic-
closing fire assembly having a fire resistance rating of
(a)
one hour for one hour fire resistive walls
(b)
one and one-half hours for two hour fire resistive walls
