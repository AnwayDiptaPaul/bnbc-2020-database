# Section 2.14.3.1: The buildings or parts thereof classified as Occupancy J shall be limited to the

2.14.3.1 The buildings or parts thereof classified as Occupancy J shall be limited to the
type of construction set forth in Table 3.2.4 and shall comply with the provisions of Sec
1.8 of Chapter 1, Part 3 and Sec 2.4.2 of this Chapter to meet the requirements of height
and area limitations.
