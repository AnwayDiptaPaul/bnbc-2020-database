# Section 2.2: Change of Use

2.2 Change of Use
2.2.1
Without prior permission from the Authorities having jurisdiction no change
shall be made in the type of occupancy or use of any building that would place it in a
different occupancy group or in a different subdivision of the same occupancy group.
Such changes shall be permitted only when the land use and the building complied with
the provisions of this Code and the laws of the land for such group of Occupancy.
