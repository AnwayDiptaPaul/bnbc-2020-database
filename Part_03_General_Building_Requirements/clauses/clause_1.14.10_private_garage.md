# Section 1.14.10: Private Garage

1.14.10 Private Garage
Private garage in residential occupancy A1 and A2 building shall have a minimum clear
height of 2.03 m. The minimum area of the parking stall in a garage shall be decided in
accordance with the provision of Sec F.7.1 of Appendix F.
