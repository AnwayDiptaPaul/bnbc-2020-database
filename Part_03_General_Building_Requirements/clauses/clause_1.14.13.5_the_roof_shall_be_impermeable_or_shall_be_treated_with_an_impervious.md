# Section 1.14.13.5: The roof shall be impermeable or shall be treated with an impervious

1.14.13.5 The roof shall be impermeable or shall be treated with an impervious
material to make it effectively water tight. Flat concrete roofs shall be topped with an
impervious layer of lime concrete or other effective means of waterproofing. All flat
roofs shall be sloped gently towards gutters, gratings or mouths of the rain water
drainage pipes.
