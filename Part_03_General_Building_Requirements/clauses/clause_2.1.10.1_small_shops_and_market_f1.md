# Section 2.1.10.1: Small Shops and Market (F1)

2.1.10.1 Small Shops and Market (F1)
These shall include any building or portion thereof with an area divided or undivided not
exceeding 300 m², used for purposes of display and sale of merchandise, either wholesale
or retail, with or without incidental storage and service facilities.
