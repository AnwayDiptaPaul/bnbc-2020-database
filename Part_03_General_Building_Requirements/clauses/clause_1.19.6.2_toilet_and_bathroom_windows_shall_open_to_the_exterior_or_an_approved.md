# Section 1.19.6.2: Toilet and bathroom windows shall open to the exterior or an approved

1.19.6.2 Toilet and bathroom windows shall open to the exterior or an approved
ventilation shaft and the operable area shall not be less than 1 m².
1.19.7
The required minimum average intensity of illumination in a habitable space at
a height of 750 mm above the floor level shall be 65 lux. Any point in a room more than
7 m away from an exterior window shall be considered to be not illuminated by daylight
unless measurement of illumination gives an intensity of 65 lux or more.
