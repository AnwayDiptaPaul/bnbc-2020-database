# Section 4.5.4.2: For buildings of occupancy B, C, D, E and I, ceiling/wall mounted fans shall be

4.5.4.2 For buildings of occupancy B, C, D, E and I, ceiling/wall mounted fans shall be
provided in each room larger than 25 m2, with a minimum of one fan every 25 m2.
Exceptions:
(a)
Corridors of buildings of all occupancies
(b)
ICU, CCU, operating theatres of Hospitals and Clinics
