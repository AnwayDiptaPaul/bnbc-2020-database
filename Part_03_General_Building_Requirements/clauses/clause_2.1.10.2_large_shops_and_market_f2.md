# Section 2.1.10.2: Large Shops and Market (F2)

2.1.10.2 Large Shops and Market (F2)
These shall include any building or portion thereof with an area divided or undivided
more than 300 m² used for purposes of display and sale of merchandise, either wholesale
or retail, with or without incidental storage and service facilities.
