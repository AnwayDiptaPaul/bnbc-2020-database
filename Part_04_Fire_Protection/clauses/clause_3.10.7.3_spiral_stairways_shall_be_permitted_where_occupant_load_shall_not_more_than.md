# Section 3.10.7.3: Spiral stairways shall be permitted where occupant load shall not more than

3.10.7.3  Spiral stairways shall be permitted where occupant load shall not more than
five. For spiral stairways the following conditions shall be applicable:
(a)
The clear width of the stairs shall not be less than 660 mm.
(b)
The height of risers shall not exceed 240 mm.
(c)
Headroom shall be not less than 1980 mm.
(d)
Treads shall have a depth not less than 190 mm at a point 300 mm from the
narrower edge.
(e)
All treads shall be identical.
