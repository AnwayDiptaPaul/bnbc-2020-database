# Section 4.6.2.3: A floor is subdivided by fire or smoke barriers and allows relocation of

4.6.2.3 A floor is subdivided by fire or smoke barriers and allows relocation of
occupants from area of incident to another area on the same floor each area shall be
considered as a zone and annunciated separately for the purpose of alarm location.
