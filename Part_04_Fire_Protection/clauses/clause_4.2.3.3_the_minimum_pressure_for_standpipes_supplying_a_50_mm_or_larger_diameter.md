# Section 4.2.3.3: The minimum pressure for standpipes supplying a 50 mm or larger diameter

4.2.3.3 The minimum pressure for standpipes supplying a 50 mm or larger diameter
hose shall be at least 300 kPa.  For standpipe supplying first aid hose (38 mm nominal
diameter) may have a minimum pressure of 200 kPa. The maximum pressure at any point
of the system shall not exceed 2434 kPa, if the hose connection at 40 mm diameter outlet
exceeds 700 kPa approved pressure regulating device shall be installed to maintain the
above maximum limits.
(The fire pump at the ground level)
