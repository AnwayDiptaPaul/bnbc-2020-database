# Section 2.12.10: Occupancy J: Hazardous

2.12.10 Occupancy J: Hazardous
(i)
Equipment and machinery in operations, igniting and/or emitting combustible
volatile substances shall be installed in a standard environment as
recommended in NFPA or equivalent standards.
(ii)
Rooms containing boiler or heating plant shall be effectively separated from
the main occupancy.
