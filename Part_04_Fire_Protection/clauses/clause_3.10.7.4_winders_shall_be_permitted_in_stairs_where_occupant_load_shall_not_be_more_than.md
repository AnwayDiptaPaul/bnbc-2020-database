# Section 3.10.7.4: Winders shall be permitted in stairs where occupant load shall not be more than

3.10.7.4  Winders shall be permitted in stairs where occupant load shall not be more than
three.
(a)
Winders shall have a tread depth not less than 150 mm and a tread depth not
less than 280 mm at a point 300 mm from the narrowest edge.
(b)
The clear width of the stairs shall not be less than 660 mm.
