# Section 3.9.7.1: Revolving doors shall be positioned with a dispersal area at a distance of 3m or

3.9.7.1 Revolving doors shall be positioned with a dispersal area at a distance of 3m or
more from the foot or top of stairway or escalators or moving walks or lift lobbies.
