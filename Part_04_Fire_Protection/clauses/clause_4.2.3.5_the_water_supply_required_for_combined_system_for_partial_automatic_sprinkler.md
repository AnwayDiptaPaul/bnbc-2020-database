# Section 4.2.3.5: The water supply required for combined system (for partial automatic sprinkler

4.2.3.5 The water supply required for combined system (for partial automatic sprinkler
and Fire Department hose) shall be calculated in accordance with Table 4.4.1 plus an
amount equal to the hydraulically calculated sprinkler demand.
