# Section 3.16.1.3: Illumination: Signs shall be internally or externally illuminated by two electric

3.16.1.3  Illumination: Signs shall be internally or externally illuminated by two electric
lamps or shall be of self-luminous type. When the luminance on the face of an exit sign is
from an external source, it shall have an intensity of not less than 53.8 lux from either
lamp. Internally illuminated signs shall provide equivalent luminance.
