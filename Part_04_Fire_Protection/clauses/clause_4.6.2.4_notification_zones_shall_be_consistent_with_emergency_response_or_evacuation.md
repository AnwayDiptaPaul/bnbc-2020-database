# Section 4.6.2.4: Notification zones shall be consistent with emergency response or evacuation

4.6.2.4 Notification zones shall be consistent with emergency response or evacuation
plan for the protected premises. The boundaries of notification zones shall be coincident
with building peripheral walls, fire or smoke compartment boundaries, floor separations
or other fire safety subdivisions.
