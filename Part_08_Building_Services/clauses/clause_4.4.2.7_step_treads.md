# Section 4.4.2.7: Step treads

4.4.2.7 Step treads
(a)
The depth of any step tread in the direction of travel shall not be less than 400
mm and the rise between treads shall not be more than 220 mm.
(b)
The maximum clearance between step treads on the horizontal run shall be 4
mm.
(c)
The tread surface of each step shall be slotted in a direction parallel to the
travel of the steps. Each slot shall not be more than 6.5 mm wide and not be
less than 9.5 mm deep; and the distance from centre to centre of adjoining
slots shall  not be more than 9.5 mm.
(d)
Safety provision shall be installed in the system to stop the escalator when
anything is stuck in the clearance between the step tread and the skirting.
