# Section 5.18.10: Flushing valve operated water closets when installed in any building shall be

5.18.10 Flushing valve operated water closets when installed in any building shall be
supplied through a separate branch pipe with a back flow preventer at the starting point
where the branch pipe is taken off from the supply pipe.
