# Section 5.23.6.3: Well cover

5.23.6.3 Well cover
All potable wells shall be equipped with a watertight cover overlapping the top of the
casing or pipe sleeve. For dug or bored well, the overlap and downward extension of the
cover shall be at least 50 mm outside the well casing or well. The annular space between
the casing or pipe sleeve and the drop pipe shall have a watertight sealing.
