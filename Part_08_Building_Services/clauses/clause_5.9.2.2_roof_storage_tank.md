# Section 5.9.2.2: Roof storage tank

5.9.2.2 Roof storage tank
The roof storage tank shall be constructed with pre-stressed or reinforced concrete or
ferro-cement or galvanized steel or of the material that will resist any action by the plain
or chlorinated water. The tank shall be made of water tight without the use of putty.
Tanks made of non-galvanized metal sheets shall be coated internally with a nontoxic
material which does not impart a taste or odor. The metal storage tank shall be coated
externally with a good quality anticorrosive weather resistant paint. The outlet of storage
tank to the distribution system shall be at least 50 mm above the tank bottom.
To provide sufficient pressure, the bottom of the tank must be elevated sufficiently above
the highest floor water fixtures.
Vent pipe should be provided to avoid any air lock and should be placed where the
horizontal branch pipes connect the vertical down feed pipes (not adjacent to storage tank
and at interconnection place between storage tank and distribution pipes).
