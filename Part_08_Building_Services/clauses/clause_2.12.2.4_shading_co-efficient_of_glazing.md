# Section 2.12.2.4: Shading co-efficient of glazing

2.12.2.4  Shading co-efficient of glazing
(a)
The shading co-efficient (SC) and solar heat gain co-efficient (SHGC) shall be
selected so as to reduce total heat influx through the glazing.
(b)
For any specific value of glazing to wall ratio (GWR) of any wall the shading co-
efficient and solar heat gain co-efficient shall be based on the Table 8.2.9(a).
